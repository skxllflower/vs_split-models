NOTICE
This pack contains ONNX exports of Meta's HT-Demucs fine-tuned models (htdemucs_ft),
released by Meta Platforms, Inc. under the MIT License reproduced below.
The ONNX export keeps the spectral transform on the host, following sevagh/demucs.onnx
(MIT). Weights are stored as float16 and computed in float32.
Packaged for WAVdesk's vs_split engine by Vacant Systems.
